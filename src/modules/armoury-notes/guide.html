What It Does

Adds shared notes to your faction's armoury items - a note, owner, category and colour per item. Everyone in your faction sees the same notes, because they are stored centrally rather than on your device.

Notes show on the Armoury, Inventory and Trade pages as a badge next to the item name; hover (or tap on mobile) the name to read the note. If your faction is part of a family, you also see items owned by other family factions, marked as loaned in.

Adding & Editing Notes (Writers)

Council and Leadership see a pencil button beside each item - click it to set the note, owner and category. Read-only members see the badges and notes but no button.

Owner - search a player by name and the picker fills in their ID. For Member Owned items you can toggle between searching your current faction members or all of Torn. Faction Owned items are set to your faction automatically.

Categories & Colours

Three categories - Faction Owned, Member Owned and Loaned From - colour-code each item. Picking a category sets its colour automatically; leave it as None to choose a custom colour instead.

Colours are shared across your whole faction and can be changed by Leadership in the settings below.

Loaned-In & Family Sharing

If your faction is in a family, an item belonging to another family faction shows a loaned-in badge with that faction's name and a ⇠ marker, in the loaned-in colour.

These are read-only: the button becomes an eye - open it to see the true owner and note. The item should be returned to that faction after use.

Retrieve & Give (Writers)

On the Armoury tab, Retrieve items and Give items stage bulk actions one click at a time. Retrieve recalls loaned-out items; Give hands available items to a chosen member.

Both let you filter by mode (loaned-in, member owned, everything), item and owner, and skip individual items. Give also takes a quantity and a recipient. The buttons only appear when Torn shows the matching action, so they respect your faction permissions.

Migrating Your Notes

Moved factions? Migrate Notes (on the Armoury tab) requests moving every note you own from other family factions into your current one. A leader of the origin faction approves it; the notes are then copied in and the originals released.

You will be able to edit them once your new faction's leadership grants you write access. Leaders see a flashing Approve migrations button when there are requests to move notes out of their faction.
