War Page - Online Visibility

On faction war pages, a collapsible Online Status bar appears showing online/idle/offline counts for both sides. Click Sort to reorder members by online status.

Configure the Mobilisation Alert to get a warning when enemy online % exceeds your threshold.

Attack Page - Button Positioning

Toggle Fight on/off to reposition attack buttons over your chosen weapon slot (Primary/Secondary/Melee). Select a Type to show only that button (Leave/Mug/Hosp/Arrest) or All.

Attack Page - Execute Alert

Toggle Execute on and set a % threshold. When the target's health drops below that percentage, the Secondary weapon slot highlights red as a visual cue to finish the fight.

Mobile HUD

On mobile attack pages, an extra HUD row shows the number of Attackers in the loader, the enemy's Loadout with weapon bonuses highlighted (Parry and Bloodlust flagged as dangers), and active Effects on you.

Vision effects like Smoked and Blinded are highlighted in red. DOT effects like Bleeding and Burning appear in orange.

Attack Page - Hospital Timer

When your target is in hospital, a countdown timer appears in the attack page header showing their remaining hospital time. A Reload button appears alongside it.

At 10 seconds remaining, the timer flashes green. If the target leaves hospital early, the timer changes to Target out of Hospital!

Profile Page - Attack Button Override

When viewing the profile of a player who is in hospital, the greyed-out Attack button is re-enabled with a blue glow (hover tooltip: "Bypass — Click to Attack") so you can click through to the attack page anyway.

War Page - Hospital Timers

On faction war pages, Hospital in the status column is replaced with a live countdown (red, turning green in the last 5 minutes). Revives and status changes are picked up automatically.

Uses two or three API calls per refresh (not one per player), only while the tab is focused and someone is in hospital. Sort still works and orders hospitalised players by who gets out first.
