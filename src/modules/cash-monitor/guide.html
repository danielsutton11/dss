How It Works

When your cash on hand exceeds the threshold, a green alert dialog appears in the top-left with your current balance and three quick actions:

Vault — navigates to your property vault and sets the max deposit, you must click deposit.

Faction Vault — navigates to the faction armoury vault and sets the max deposit, you must click deposit.

Trade — navigates to the trade page, for you to add to a ghost trade.

Alert Behaviour

Dismissing the alert (the × button or Dismiss button) starts the cooldown set above. The cooldown is shared across all open tabs, so dismissing in one tab silences the alert everywhere.

This module runs on every Torn page since cash is always visible in the sidebar.
