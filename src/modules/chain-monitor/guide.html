Chain Bar Alerts

The chain bar pulses with colour-coded alerts as time runs down. Alerts only activate when the chain count is 10 or higher.

Green — gentle pulse. Orange — faster pulse. Red — rapid flash with target dialog.

Random Target Dialog

When the timer hits red, a dialog appears in the bottom-left with a random target ID. Click Attack to open the attack page, or New Target to roll a different ID.

Always Active

This module runs on every Torn page since the chain bar is always visible. Alerts and the target dialog only show while the tab is focused.
