Background Image URL

Enter the direct URL to your background image. Use Catbox.moe or Imgur for best quality.

How It Works

The image is applied as a full-screen fixed background with cover sizing. Torn's default background elements are hidden.

A blur overlay is applied over the content column so text remains readable against the image.

The overlay scales automatically with browser zoom level.

Notes

Changes take effect on the next page load after saving. To remove the background, click Clear and reload.

For best results, use a high-resolution image (1920x1080 or larger).
