What It Does

Desktop: while you're in hospital, a red MEDX REVIVE ME button appears under your life bar in the sidebar, and a green link is added to the top page links next to NITE, on every page. On a hospitalised player's profile the link reads MEDX Revive Player and requests a revive for that player instead.

Phone / Torn PDA: a round red button floats in the bottom-right corner while you're in hospital, or while you're viewing a hospitalised player's profile.

One press sends the request. The button turns grey while sending and green for 10 seconds once Med X accepts it, so a double tap can't send twice.

Layout

The layout is picked automatically from your device. If it guesses wrong (for example on a tablet or a touchscreen laptop), set it here.

Payment

Revives cost 2 Xanax or $1.8m per attempt, paid to your reviver.

Third-Party Data Sharing

Each request sends the API key from your DSS Setup page, your Torn ID and username, and the ID of the player to revive to medx.fun, the Med X revive service. Nothing is sent until you press a Revive button, and nothing is sent at all while this module is disabled.
